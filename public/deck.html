02 / 11 The problem

Talented students lose to bad information

A student in Kazakhstan who could get into MIT often doesn't — not for lack of ability, but because nobody told them what to do, or when.

Scattered

Olympiads, grants and scholarships live across dozens of sites, Telegram channels and Instagram posts. No single source, nothing verified.

Missed

Deadlines pass silently. One missed date means waiting a full year for the next cycle.

Unaffordable

Private admission consultants charge $500–2,000 per student — out of reach for most families here.

Directionless

"What should I even study?" gets answered by a relative's opinion, not by evidence about the student.

03 / 11 The solution

One place, from "I don't know" to submitted

Lumifield AI replaces a $2,000 consultant with an AI that knows the student, knows the local landscape, and never forgets a deadline.

Know yourself

A 20-question survey turns grades, subjects, interests and constraints into a real direction — not a horoscope.

Know the options

A verified catalog of opportunities: olympiads, grants, internships and summer schools, local and international.

Know the plan

A roadmap by semester with tracked deadlines, diagnostic tests and admission-chance estimates.

04 / 11 How it works

Four steps, one evening

1

Answer 20 questions

Grades, strong and weak subjects, language certificates, interests, budget, target country and year.

2

Get the AI report

Careers matched to the profile, real universities with requirements and deadlines, a motivation letter draft, reading list.

3

Follow the roadmap

The plan adapts to the student's year — grade 9 through second-year university — with concrete opportunities at each phase.

4

Apply on time

Deadline tracker, materials checklist, diagnostic IELTS/SAT/HSK scores, and an AI assistant for the essay.
